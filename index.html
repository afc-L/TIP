STEEL & BLOOD

A physics-driven medieval duel. Every swing has weight. Every mistake bleeds.

Best with a mouse. Click the arena to lock the cursor; Esc releases it.

How to fight

  1. Your mouse is your sword hand. Moving it moves the hand in front of your body — up, down, left, right. The blade follows physically, with real momentum. Mouse wheel / ↑↓ change how far you reach.
  2. Grip — hold Left Mouse. Without it your arm is relaxed and slow. Hold LMB and sweep the mouse: wind up high on one side, then drag hard through your opponent. A fast sweep is a powerful cut — and hard to stop.
  3. Edge alignment is automatic — the edge turns into the direction of motion. Hit with the edge to cut, the point (F: thrust) to stab, or the flat to bludgeon. Slow pushes do nothing.
  4. Guard — hold V. Raises the blade across your body; move the mouse to cover high/low/left/right. Q snaps your blade into the incoming weapon (parry) — time it well and the attacker is jarred.
  5. Balance matters. Big swings, hits and collisions knock you off balance. Hold Space (standing still) to brace and recover. Space while moving = dodge step.
  6. If you fall, press Space to get back up when you have the stamina. E grabs the opponent with your free hand (E again to let go). X toggles a two-handed grip: stronger, steadier swings, but no free hand. Shift to run.
  7. Armor stops cuts but not crushing blows. Injured arms grow weak, injured legs slow you, wounds bleed. Fights end when someone stops moving.
