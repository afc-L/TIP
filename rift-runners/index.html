Survive the Rift

  • Clear 10 waves. Wave 10 is the Rift Guardian – defeat it to win.
  • Enemies drop experience crystals. Level up to choose 1 of 3 upgrades.
  • Unlock Arc Burst and Void Orb as upgrades. Holding fire uses every weapon.
  • Combine upgrades to trigger synergies – e.g. Ignite + Piercing Rounds = Wildfire.
  • Red / orange zones on the ground are incoming attacks. Dash out of them!
  • Bosses pause after big attacks – that's your window to strike.
  • Earn Rift Shards each run and spend them in the Sanctum.
  • Prefer not to hold the mouse? Turn on Auto-fire in Settings.
