Survive the Rift

  • Clear 10 waves: a random mid-boss guards wave 5, the Rift Guardian waits on wave 10.
  • Level up to pick upgrades. Hold up to 4 weapons and 4 relics.
  • A weapon at level 4 plus the right relic evolves. Check the Collection for recipes.
  • Every 2–3 waves a Rift Event changes the rules. Watch the preview between waves.
  • Elites have coloured outlines and modifier icons; they drop gold and sometimes relics.
  • Coloured zones on the ground are incoming attacks. Dash out of them!
  • Bosses pause after big attacks — that's your window. Hard and Nightmare unlock new tricks.
  • Beat the campaign to unlock Endless and Nightmare.
