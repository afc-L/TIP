How to play

You are the monster. Everything it physically does comes from you. The game will never move it, turn it, or make it attack. But it will lie to your eyes and ears.

People notice movement, light and noise. Darkness and crouching hide you. Panic spreads: a scream brings everyone's attention. Some people hide. Some call for help — and help comes with lights. Some fight back.

People cannot hear or see the things that are only in your head. Watching how they react is the only way to be sure. Mostly.
