SURVIVAL
  • You came to Blackpine to hunt. Track deer by their prints, approach quietly, and aim for the head or heart for a clean kill. Wounded animals leave a blood trail.
  • Watch your health, hunger and flashlight battery. Search cabins, towers and abandoned gear for supplies.
  • Nights are long and dark. A lit campfire is the safest place in the forest. Sleep in your tent to skip the night and save.
  • If the birds stop singing, something is close. If you see something in the trees, it has already seen you.
  • Light drives it back. Gunfire staggers it. Running buys time.
  • There is more than one way out of Blackpine — and what you find, and how much you take, decides how it ends.
